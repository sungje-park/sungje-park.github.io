# About Me

{{< about-intro photo="images/sungje-park.jpg" >}}

Hello! I am an Electrical Engineering PhD student at USC, advised by <a href="https://stephentu.github.io" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Dr. Stephen Tu</a>, and an NSF Graduate Research Fellow.

I am broadly interested in developing safe and robust methods for learning-based autonomous systems. My current research focuses on deep learning methods inspired by stochastic differential equations for solving partial differential equations, with applications to Hamilton–Jacobi reachability.

Before joining USC, I studied Aerospace Engineering at Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University, with minors in Applied Mathematics and Computer Science, and worked with <a href="https://faculty.erau.edu/Leitao.Chen" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Dr. Leitao Chen</a> and <a href="https://faculty.erau.edu/Vladimir.Golubev" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Dr. Vladimir Golubev</a>.

{{< /about-intro >}}

Outside of USC, I am best spotted pedaling up the hills of LA, searching for fish along riverbanks and lakeshores, and trekking up alpine trails. In the winter months, I migrate to the nearest snowy peaks whenever time and weather permit.
