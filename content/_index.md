## About Me

Hello! My name is Sungje Park and I am an Electrical Engineering PhD student at USC advised by <a href="https://stephentu.github.io" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Dr. Stephen Tu</a>. I am broadly interested in machine learning for scientific computing, with a focus on learning-based approaches to mathematical problems in safe autonomy. My recent work revolves around stochastic differential equation (SDE)-inspired deep learning methods for solving partial differential equations (PDEs), with applications to Hamilton–Jacobi (HJ) reachability.

Prior to joining USC, I studied Aerospace Engineering (aeronautics) at ERAU with minors in Applied Math and Computer Science. During my time at ERAU, I had the privilege of working with <a href="https://faculty.erau.edu/Leitao.Chen" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Dr. Leitao Chen</a> and <a href="https://faculty.erau.edu/Vladimir.Golubev" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Dr. Vladimir Golubev</a> on Boltzmann-based computational fluid dynamics (CFD) for porous xmulti-component flows and reinforcement learning for dynamic soaring on UAVs.

Outside of USC, I am best spotted pedaling up the hills of LA, searching for fish along riverbanks and lakeshores, and trekking up alpine trails. In the winter months, I migrate to the nearest snowy peaks whenever time and weather permit.

The best way to reach me is via email @ sungjepa (at) usc (dot) edu.
